# The Five-Minute Reset

A training space fails when it asks you to clean before you can start. The reset is a short habit that happens after the session, not a weekend project. 

## The five minutes

- Roll or fold the mat
- Put bands or dumbbells back in the same spot
- Wipe the water ring
- Move laundry, bags, and mail off the floor 

Stop at five minutes. If it takes longer, the space has too much in it. Take one thing out of the corner instead of organizing harder. 

## Why it matters on tired days

The next session starts in the state you left. A clear floor is a yes. A pile is a negotiation. This is how [[A Corner That Counts]] stays usable in week six, not just on the day you set it up. 

After the reset, note the session in [[The Weekly Check-In]] so the room and the record match.
