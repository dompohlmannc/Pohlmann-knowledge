# A Corner That Counts

You do not need a home gym to start. You need a spot you can find without negotiating with the rest of the house. A corner of the bedroom, the end of the living room, or a clear patch of garage floor is enough if it stays clear. 

## What belongs there

- A mat or towel you do not have to hunt for
- One pair of shoes that are only for moving
- A water bottle that lives in that corner, not in the sink
- Whatever you will actually use this week: a band, a pair of dumbbells, or nothing. 

This is the same idea as [[The Five-Minute Reset]]]]: the room should make the next session easier than skipping it. When the corner works, [[Shoes and a Water Bottle]] is the only packing list you need. 

>A small space you can use beats a perfect room you avoid.
