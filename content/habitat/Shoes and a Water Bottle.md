# Shoes and a Water Bottle

Most missed sessions are not a motivation problem. They are a friction problem. If the shoes are in a closet and the bottle is dirty, the workout has already lost. 

## The leave-it-ready rule

The night before, or right after you get home, put three things where you will see them: 

1. Shoes
2. A filled water bottle
3. The clothes you will wear

That is the whole system. No new wardrobe. No color-coded bins. If you train outside the house, the bag y the door is the same idea. 

## When you train at home 

Keep the shoes next to [[A Corner That Counts]]. You are less likely to talk yourself out of ten minutes if the first step is already done.
