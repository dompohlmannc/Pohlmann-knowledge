# What to Keep in Sight

You will not follow a plan you cannot see. The habitat part of this knowledge base is mostly about cues: objects and notes that sit where your eyes already go. 

## Useful cues

- Shoes by the door or by the mat
- A water bottles that is already full
- One written line on the fridge or mirror, from [[Small-Step Mantras]]
- The weekly note from [[The Weekly Check-In]], not a stack of old plans
