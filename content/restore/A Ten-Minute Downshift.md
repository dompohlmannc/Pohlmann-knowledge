# A Ten-Minute Downshift

Restore does not have to be a long routine. Ten quiet minutes is enough to mark the day as finished so the next one can start. 

## A simple version

1. Sit or lie down where you will not scroll. 
2. Unclench your jaw and drop your shoulders.
3. Breather slower than your default for a few minutes. 
4. Stretch only what feels tight. Stop if it becomes a performance. 

That is the session. You can do it after a walk, after work, or instead of a workout you do not have. It pairs with [[Sleep Is Part of the Plan]] because a downshift is often what makes the bedtime real.
