# Sleep Is Part of the Plan

Training on short sleep feels productive until the week falls apart. Sleep is not a reward you earn after the workout. It is part of being able to repeat the workout. 

## A floor, not a perfect night

Aim for a bedtime you can hit four nights, not an ideal eight hours you announce and miss. The floor can be simple: 
- Phone out of the bed
- Same wind-down start, even if it is only fifteen minutes
- Caffeine stopped early
