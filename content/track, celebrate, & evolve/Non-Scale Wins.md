# Non-Scale Wins

The scale is one number. It is poor weekly report. A non-scale win is evidence that small steps are happening. 

## Wins that count

- You started a session you almost skipped
- You came back after a miss without restarting
- Your built a plate with protein on a busy night
- A walk felt easier than last time. 
- You kept the corner clear

If you cannot name a win, the week was too vague. Shrink next week down to make it more attainable, but do not give up.
