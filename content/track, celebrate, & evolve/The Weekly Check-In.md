# The Weekly Check-In

Once a week, look at what you actually did. Not the plan you wish you had followed. The check-in is short so you will still do it in a busy week. 

## Four lines

1. Sessions you started, including walks
2. Meals that followed the plate, even roughly
3. Sleep; enough, or thin
4. One thing to repeat next week

Keep it in the same note every Sunday, or whichever day you already pause.
