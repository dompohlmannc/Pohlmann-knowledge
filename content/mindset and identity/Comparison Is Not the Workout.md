# Comparison Is Not the Workout

Someone else's highlight is not your session. Comparison feels like information. Most of the time it is a reason to delay. 

## A cleaner measure

Compare today to your last ordinary week, not to a stranger's best angle. Did you start? Did you eat in a way you can repeat? Did you sleep enough to train again Those are the questions in [[Non-Scale Wins]].

## When the scroll starts

Put the phone in another room for the session. If you want a model, use your own note from last month. Progress you cannot see in a mirror still counts: a walk that used to feel long, a meal that you assembled without a spiral, a week you came back after a miss.
