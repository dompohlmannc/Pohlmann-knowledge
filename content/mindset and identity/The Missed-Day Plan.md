# The Missed-Day Plan

A missed day is not a failed plan. It is a day that needs a smaller door back in. Decide the door before you need it. 

## The rule

If you miss a session, the next session is the shortest version you already trust. Ten minutes. A walk. The plate from [[Building  Your Plate| The Plate]], not a new diet. You do not "make up" the missed work by doubling the next day. 

## What not to do

- Restart on Monday
- Punish the miss with a harder workout
- Hide the miss so the log looks clean
