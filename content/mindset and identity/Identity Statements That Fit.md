# Identity Statements That Fit

"I am a fitness person" is too big to use on a Tuesday. A statement that fits is something you can prove this week. 

## Statements that hold up

- I am someone who starts, even if the session is short. 
- I am someone who eats protein before I Get frantic.
- I am someone who comes back after a missed day. 

Pick one. Say it in the present tense. If it feels like a costume, shrink it until it is true.
