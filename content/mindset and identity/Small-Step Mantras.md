# Small-Step Mantras

A mantra here is a short line you can use while you are deciding, not a speech. If you would not say it out loud in the kitchen, it is too polished. 

## Lines that work

- Start smaller. 
- Protein first. 
- Come back tomorrow. 
- The walk counts. 
- Done is enough today. 

Pick two. Put one where you would get ready and one where you eat. That is [[What to Keep in Sight]], not a new personality.
