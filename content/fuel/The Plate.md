# Building Your Plate

![[Fat Loss Plate.jpg]]

You do not need a perfect macro spreadsheet to start eating in a way that supports your training. You need a plate you can assemble half-asleep. The rule here is small and repeatable: **protein, color, and a carb that matches the day you are actually having.**

## The three-part plate

### Protein First

Put a palm or two of protein down before anything else. Chicken, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, leftover steak. Protein is what keeps the meal from turning into a sugar crash two hours later and what helps you recover from the work you do in the gym. 

### Color second

Vegetables and fruit are not a personality. They are volume, fiber, and micronutrients that make the plate feel more like a meal. Frozen broccoli counts. A handful of berries counts. 

#### Carbs that match the day

Training day, long walk, or physically heavy shift: keep the rice, potato, tortilla, or fruit. Rest day or a short evening: You can go lighter without turning dinner into a salad you resent. 

That is the whole method. [[Protein Bowls You Will Repeat|Protein bowls]] are just this plate in a bowl. [[Grocery Staples]] exist so those four lines are not a scavenger hunt.

>Small steps look boring on paper. They are how people still eat well in week six.
