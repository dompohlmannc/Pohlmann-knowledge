# Protein Bowls You Will Repeat

A bowl is a plate that forgives you. Everything lands in one dish, the toppings do the flavor work, and you can eat it cold from the fridge if the day ran long. This is the format I come back to for clients and for my own kitchen: high protein, built from staples, not from a 30 ingredient "wellness" recipe.

! [A Simple high-protein bowl with yogurt or cottage cheese, fruit, and toppings](https://hungryhealthyhappy.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/chicken-protein-bowl-finished-1.jpg)

*A bowl does not need to look styled. It just needs simple protein, something fresh, and a topping you like enough to make it again.*

### Savory bowl

Base: leftover rice, a microwave packet of grains, or chopped greens. 
Protein: rotisserie chicken, turkey, taco meat, or beans 
Color: Salsa, cucumber, leftover veggies.
Finish: Hot honey, chipotle, or a spoon of greek yogurt as sauce 

### Cottage cheese or yogurt bowl

Base: low-fat cottage cheese or plain Greek yogurt. (Protein is already handled)
Color: Berries, tomato and cucumber, or pineapple.
Finish: Bagel seasoning for savory, cinnamon and hot honey for sweet.
