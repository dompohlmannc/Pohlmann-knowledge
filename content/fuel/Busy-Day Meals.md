# Busy-Day Meals

Some days the workout happens and the kitchen does not. On those days, the goal isn't a home-cooked masterpiece. The goal is **food with protein in it before you get so hungry that the drive-through chooses for you.**

## A twenty-minute ceiling

If dinner cannot be done in twenty minutes, it does not belong on a busy-day list. That includes cleanup. You may use the microwave, the rotisserie chicken, and the yogurt lid. 

### Default dinners

- Eggs and toast plus a handful of fruit or salsa
- Rotisserie chicken on a tortilla with whatever vegetable is left
- Greek yogurt or cottage cheese bowl from the [[fuel/protein bowls|bowl templates]]
- Frozen steam bag vegetables plus a protein you already cooked

## Decision order when you are super tired

1. What protein is already cooked or ready to eat?
2. What color is in the fridge or freezer?
3. Do I need a carb because I trained?
4. What flavor makes me willing to sit down and eat it?
