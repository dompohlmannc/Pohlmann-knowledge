# Grocery Staples That Earn Their Shelf

A "healthy kitchen" that requires a second store and a specialty spice you will use once is not a system. Staples are the foods that show up in more than one meal and still look like food when you are tired. 

![[Meal-Prep-101-Handout.pdf]]

## The Shopping Cart

### Protein that waits for you

- Eggs
- Greek yogurt or cottage cheese
- A rotisserie chicken or a pack of chicken thighs
- Canned Tuna
- A bag of frozen edamame

These turn into breakfast, lunch, or dinner a [[Protein Bowls You Will Repeat|Protein Bowl]] or the center of [[fuel/building-your-plate|the plate]] without a new recipe. 

### Produce that survives a week

- Frozen Vegetables (Broccoli, spinach, etc.) This is how our vegetables last until Thursday.
- Add a fresh fruit you will actually eat. 
- Grab a crunchy vegetable that you will snack on and not let die in the crisper drawer. 

### Flavor without the alphabet

Salsa, hot honey, mustard, soy sauce, lemon, garlic powder, and one hot sauce will carry more meals than a dozen half-used jars.
